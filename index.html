Vice Run Support

A neon arcade runner with a turquoise scooter, five locations, and one more chance to beat your best.

Contact support

Email alexcaphu@icloud.com for help, bug reports, or privacy questions.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, and a description of what happened. A screenshot is helpful. Do not send passwords, Apple ID credentials, or payment details.

How to play

Tap to start. A short tap makes a short jump; hold for a longer jump. Avoid obstacles and try to beat your best score. Tap the speaker icon to turn audio on or off.

Scores and accounts

No account or sign-in is required. Your best score and audio preference are saved locally on your device. This release does not use online leaderboards or Game Center.

Optional ad continuation

Advertising-enabled versions offer WATCH AD · CONTINUE after a crash, at most once per run. A continuation is granted only when the advertising SDK confirms the reward. You can always start a new run without watching an ad. If required, AD PRIVACY OPTIONS lets you revisit your advertising choices.

If something goes wrong

Close and reopen the game, then check for an available update. If the problem continues, contact support. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting: doing so can remove your local best score.

Read the Privacy Policy for details about local saves, support emails, and this website.
